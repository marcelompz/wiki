# OmniFlow Trademark & Brand Policy

## 1. Purpose and Philosophy
The open-source release of the OmniFlow core empowers developers and businesses to inspect, self-host, and contribute to the platform. However, to protect our community and users from confusion, misrepresentation, or substandard counterfeits, the trademarks and brand assets associated with OmniFlow are subject to this Trademark Policy.

While the source code is released under an open-source license (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)), this license grants rights **only to the software code and explicitly excludes trademark and brand rights**.

## 3. What You CAN Do (Permitted Uses)
Without prior written consent, you may:
* **Accurate Identification:** Refer to the project truthfully in conversation, articles, documentation, or blog posts (e.g., *"A tutorial on integrating PostgreSQL with OmniFlow"* or *"How to deploy OmniFlow on Linux"*).
* **Statement of Compatibility:** State that your own independently developed software or extension is compatible with the platform (e.g., *"Extension XYZ for OmniFlow"* or *"Designed to work with OmniFlow"*), provided that:
  * Your product name does **not** begin with "Omni" in a way that suggests it is an official vertical module.
  * You clearly display a disclaimer: *"This project is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by OmniFlow."*
* **Unmodified Binaries / Official Forks:** Distribute exact, verbatim copies of the official source code, provided all copyright notices remain intact.

## 4. What You CANNOT Do (Restricted Uses)
Unless you have received express, prior written authorization:
* **Modified Forks and Re-distributions:** If you modify the source code, fork the project, or build a derivative platform, **you must remove all OmniFlow trademarks, logos, and branding** before distributing or hosting it publicly. You cannot distribute a modified version under the name "OmniFlow" or any confusingly similar variation.
* **Commercial SaaS / Managed Services:** You may not offer a hosted service, managed deployment, or SaaS product named "OmniFlow [Service]", "Omni Gastro Cloud", or similar, that falsely implies an official or endorsed commercial offering.
* **Brand Confusion & Domain Names:** You may not register domain names, social media handles, or company names containing "OmniFlow" or "Omni Gastro", nor use our logos as part of your company or product identity.
* **Prefixed Module Imitation:** You may not use the "Omni [Domain]" naming pattern (such as "Omni POS", "Omni Retail", or "Omni Gastro") in a manner intended to confuse customers into believing your add-on or service is an official vertical module from the core creators.
